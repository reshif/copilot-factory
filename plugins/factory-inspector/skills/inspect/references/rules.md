# Reading inspector findings by rule

Read this when the report lists a health finding, a clarify or grounded-intent finding, or the user asks what a rule means. Rule ids are in each finding; `factory-inspect report --format md` lists the ones that fired.

The health rules count what the factory reports about itself against limits in `.factory/inspect/config.json` (`limits`, `rules_off`). A **hook error** on the guard, Stop or prompt hook is high severity: while it failed, that hook allowed everything — say what could have slipped through in that window. For repeated guard denials, refused or look-alike replies, CLI failures, stale results, degraded steps, agent retries/failures/escalations, parks and resumes, explain the pattern and the likely cause from the evidence. For clarify parks (R19.clarify_parked), say whether agents failed (time caps, the model) or a cap stopped work that was converging; for God Eye objections the user proceeded past (R19.proceeded_past_objections), say what God Eye objected to and that H1 showed it as an objection, not approval. For research gaps (R20.research_gaps), say what research could not settle and why (web off, no web access, no page for the confirmed version, a time box too small for the risk). Older jobs: for intent parks and dropped clarify questions (R19), say whether the request or the judge looks at fault. For grounded intent (R20): questions asked although research could have answered them — give the reason it recorded (web off by policy is a setting the user may revisit; no web access, sources that disagree or a spent budget point at the environment or the research budget); assumptions the user changed or dropped are a learning signal — say what the research claimed and what the user decided instead, and whether the evidence looks wrong or the point was the user's call all along.
