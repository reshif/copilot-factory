# Reading inspector findings by rule

Read this when the report lists a health finding, a clarify or grounded-intent finding, or the user asks what a rule means. Rule ids are in each finding; `factory-inspect report --format md` lists the ones that fired.

The health rules count what the factory reports about itself against limits in `.factory/inspect/config.json` (`limits`, `rules_off`). A **hook error** on the guard, Stop or prompt hook is high severity: while it failed, that hook allowed everything — say what could have slipped through in that window. For repeated guard denials, refused or look-alike replies, CLI failures, stale results, degraded steps, agent retries/failures/escalations, parks and resumes, explain the pattern and the likely cause from the evidence. For intent parks and dropped clarify questions (R19), say whether the request or the judge looks at fault. For grounded intent (R20): questions asked although research could have answered them — give the reason it recorded (web off by policy is a setting the user may revisit; no web access, sources that disagree or a spent budget point at the environment or the research budget); assumptions the user changed or dropped are a learning signal — say what the research claimed and what the user decided instead, and whether the evidence looks wrong or the point was the user's call all along.
